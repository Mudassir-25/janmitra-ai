How JanMitra works

From a citizen’s words to a government-ready action queue, while keeping Gemini keys server-side.

Citizen reports

Free text in English, Hindi, Kannada or Tamil.Browser → POST /api/analyze

Gemini analyses

Server-side model extracts category, state, urgency, and impacted people.Structured JSON and no key exposure

Score and validate

Outputs are checked against valid values and turned into a 0–100 priority score.Demo Mode uses the same logic

Officials act

Requests aggregate into map, heatmap, queue, and recommendations.Human review remains in the loop

Priority score (0–100)

Service criticality (up to 22) + urgency (up to 30) + people affected on a logarithmic scale (up to 30) + vulnerability factors such as children, hospitals, and elderly residents (up to 18).

Responsible use

AI ranks and explains; officials decide. Scores require field verification, and non-digital channels remain important for equitable access. This prototype uses synthetic data only.

Languages

Prompting can extend to all scheduled languages, with voice intake as the next step for lower-literacy users.

Channels

WhatsApp, IVR, and Common Service Centres can feed the same endpoint so citizens without smartphones still participate.

Integration

Grievance portals and GIS ward boundaries can increase location precision and reduce duplicate reports.

Cloud

Stateless container runtime, secret manager for Gemini keys, and auditable logs for every AI decision.

Fairness

Monitor whether remote or low-literacy areas are under-ranked and adjust weights to preserve equity.

Cost

A short model call per request keeps the system cost-efficient while maintaining locally relevant prioritization.
